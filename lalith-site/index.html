Research

DiffFind: Discovering Differential Equations from Time Series

Lalithsai Posam, Shubhranshu Shekhar, Meng-Chieh Lee, Christos Faloutsos

PAKDD 2024 · Lecture Notes in Computer Science, vol. 14650

DiffFind uses genetic algorithms to find the differential equations behind a time series, including nonlinear systems that linear methods miss. It needs no manual tuning, and it ranked first or second against autoregression and SINDy on all five datasets we tested. I did this work with Christos Faloutsos's group at Carnegie Mellon.
